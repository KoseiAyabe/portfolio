HISTORY

Let's explore my life!

1997

Born in Shizuoka

Born in a small town called Kannami in eastern Shizuoka.
As a kid, I was always outside or playing video games. Got hooked on skateboarding in junior and senior high.
I played volleyball in school clubs and was good at academics—at least until junior high.
Around that time, I started vaguely dreaming about running my own business someday.

2016

Entered IT Faculty

As a middle schooler, I was blown away when I first got my hands on an iPod Touch and iPhone.
Fascinated by the rapid spread of the internet, social media, and smartphone apps, I decided I wanted to become an engineer.
In high school, I was fully focused on sports and my grades slipped badly,
but I buckled down for entrance exams and managed to get into my top-choice university.

2020

Graduated and Started Career

Graduated and joined a major system integration company as a new graduate.
Just as I started my career, COVID-19 hit and I began working fully remotely.
Over the next two and a half years, I moved around Japan—Chiba, Kumamoto, Hiroshima.

2022

Became a Freelancer

Having joined the company to build my professional experience, I left after about two and a half years.
I had always wanted to work in Tokyo, so I moved there after quitting and started out as a freelance engineer.
I soon realized that technical skills alone weren’t enough to stand out in the freelance world.
After a lot of soul-searching, I set my sights on learning English and working as an engineer abroad.

2024

Studied in Cebu

While continuing to study English alongside freelance work, I hit a wall with growing in Japan.
I made the decision to study abroad and spent three months in Cebu, Philippines.
Living overseas for the first time and connecting with international friends was so much fun
that I decided to go on a working holiday the following year.

2025

Working Holiday in Canada

With Silicon Valley—the heart of global tech—close by in the U.S., I decided Canada was the ideal place to grow both my technical and English skills.
I started living in Vancouver in July 2025.
By a stroke of luck, I landed an engineering job in December 2025,
marking the start of my long-awaited career as an engineer abroad.
